# ⭐ STAR Story Bank

> Write each story once, rehearse aloud, reuse for many questions. Target: **10 stories by Week 18**.
> Format: **S**ituation (1–2 lines) · **T**ask (your responsibility) · **A**ction (what *you* did — 60% of the answer) · **R**esult (numbers + learning).

## Prompts to mine stories from your current job
1. The hardest bug / production issue you fixed.
2. A time you improved performance (query, stored procedure, API, batch job).
3. A disagreement with a teammate or manager — how it was resolved.
4. A mistake or failure — what you changed afterwards.
5. Ownership beyond your role (automation, documentation, onboarding someone).
6. Learning something new fast under a deadline.
7. Handling unclear requirements / pushing back on scope.
8. Mentoring or helping a teammate.
9. A decision with trade-offs (speed vs quality, quick fix vs refactor).
10. **Why are you switching?** (positive framing: growth in backend/cloud/AI — never complain about the employer)
